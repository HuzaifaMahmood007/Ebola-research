## 3. The spatial story, final form (for the manuscript)

Chain, every link tested: district signal exists in the raw data (t6) -> the encoder compresses
it 3-5x (t5) -> mean aggregation deletes most of the rest (t3/t4) -> training barely needs the
real map, dengue the one small exception (D2) -> forcing the signal back in via a deviation
branch makes the model roughly twice as bad under a pre-registered test (V2). So the compression
is not a defect to fix; it behaves like a defence against noise-dominated deviations. FENCES:
the exact V2 failure mechanism (overfit vs regime shift) is not established; the V1 COVID nulls
(gate-off, D2) at 5 seeds mean "no effect of ~12-15% or more", state it that way; a linear probe
CAN use the deviations, a 10k-parameter branch cannot.
