# Final Audit: Disease-Agnostic ST-GNN, Cross-Disease Transfer, Held-Out Ebola

Nine dimensions audited against code and data; every material finding attacked by an independent skeptic. What follows is what survived.

## Executive verdict

**Datasets:** right sources. Dengue (OpenDengue V1.3), the ColaGNN influenza matrices and the two frozen Ebola arms are the choices a reviewer expects, and their audited figures reproduce exactly from `data/processed/*.npz`. COVID was added as a fifth training panel and the authoritative audit still denies it exists. **Representation:** adequate as a *schema*, not as a *scale* — the 4-channel transfer surface is enforced in three places and machine-checked, but Ebola runs on one pooled scaler and dengue's held-out z-space carries a 73-sigma tail. **Splits:** the leakage layer is clean; I found no target-leakage path, masks partition the observed cells exactly, and the gates are mutation-tested with planted leaks. The three LDO3 folds nonetheless do not isolate disease. **Measurement:** no. The metric primitives are the best part of the codebase; above them the Ebola headline and the interval that adjudicates it are different statistics, two significance rules disagree on 27 of 64 cells, and half of pre-registered E6 was never run. **Overall:** the engineering and the pre-registration are strong; the inference layer and the write-up are not.

## What went right

This is half the deliverable and it is not padding — several of these are stronger than field norm.

**The Ebola mass-conservation repair is real and independently checkable.** Recomputed from `data/processed/ebola_L12.npz`: total observed mass 24,552 equals the sum of the per-district `cumulative_reference` in meta, to the case, and the national peak lands on 2014-10-25 at 2,688 cases/week — against 2015-02-14 / 4,982 in the clipped build (`data_audit.md:1031-1053`). The reference is computed by a separate routine from the source column, which is what makes the check non-circular (`data_audit.md:1055-1059`). The single most damaging defect the project ever had (35.8% of the Ebola target fabricated, the peak displaced by three months) is genuinely fixed, and a reviewer can verify it without rerunning the build.

**The pre-registration is real, not nominal.** Both arms' content sha256 and file hashes match `configs/ebola_arms.json` (L12 content `08d657dc…`, L20 `e9b9ac0b…`). `freeze_ebola_arms.py:120-152` gates each arm before writing on mask partition, calendar causality, cutoff date, support-column count, scaler-fit-on-support-only and every pre-registered pair count; `:161-164` refuses to run on a drifted source hash. `train/ebola.py:58-73` re-hashes at score time and refuses to score if anything moved. Every split count in `Ebola_Prereg.md:56-61` reproduces line-for-line from the frozen tensors (L12 = 59 support cells / 18 districts, adaptation pairs 48/38/18/0). Amendment A1 (`Ebola_Prereg.md:219`) corrected the evaluation size from 1,151/1,075/866/642 to 757/766/765/642 *before* the run, "by reading the scoring path before running it, not after" — a 34–52% overstatement caught by process.

**The pre-registration then earned its keep by falsifying itself.** E5 (`Ebola_Prereg.md:182-186`) was the designed-to-be-informative cell and was cleanly falsified: L20 few-shot is 51.58 and 40.85 at h10/h15 against L12's 41.19 and 28.52 (`Priority_Fix_Progress.md:670-673`). E1, stated at "high" confidence — "zero-shot loses to persistence at every horizon, on both arms" — was falsified in the project's favour, with the bootstrap CI clearing zero on both arms at h15 (`ebola_bootstrap.log:147-154`). This is what pre-registration is for.

**The influenza provenance work is better than the source papers'.** `data_audit.md:651-671` claims `region785.txt` *is* ILINet `num_ili` anchored 2002-10-05 and `state360.txt` is anchored 2010-10-09. I reconfirmed both against the CDC/Delphi API cell-for-cell: region785 row 368 = [1883, 4780, 15928, 9814, 11269] matches `fluview` hhs1–hhs5 at epiweek 200942 exactly; state360 row 0 matches AL/AK/AZ/AR/CA/CO at 201040 exactly, confirming both the alphabetical-less-Florida ordering and the corrected October anchor. The 39-week anchor error would have put every seasonality channel ~180° out of phase on all three influenza panels and no row-count test could have caught it. The anchor is re-asserted at build time (`loaders/influenza_load.py:148-154`), not merely documented.

**The disease-agnostic claim is enforced in code, not asserted in prose.** `bundles.py:111-122` slices `core_feature_idx`, which is `[0,1,2,3]` on all eight npz files; `models/encoder.py:58` asserts `Z.shape[-1] == 4`; `tests/test_encoder_invariants.py:55-67` proves a 5-channel input raises and that `forward` has no `C` argument. Ebola's `deaths_norm` channel is structurally unreachable. `node_indexed_params(SharedEncoder())` returns `[]` — 142,305 trunk parameters, none with any dimension in `NODE_COUNTS = {10,47,49,61,7165}` (`models/config.py:12`), zero buffers — and the test plants an `nn.Parameter(47)` negative control that fires (`tests/test_encoder_invariants.py:34-39`). The same weight set ran at N=47, 49 and 7,165 with no reshape. No model in the competitive table does this.

**The encoder-family filter (P1) was the right call and the whole project depends on it.** `Encoder Decision.md:9` disqualifies Graph WaveNet / MTGNN / AGCRN because their node-embedding dictionaries `E ∈ R^{N×d}` are transductive and size-locked, and `:11` shows EpiGNN is size-locked via `W^s ∈ R^{N×N}`. That constraint became a code-level gate rather than a discipline. It was independently vindicated when HeatGNN NaN'd on degree-0 influenza nodes for lacking the self-loop-before-normalisation rule (`decisions.md:106-119`).

**The leakage suite proves its own gates can fail.** `tests/test_leakage.py:97-103` poisons held-out cells 1000× and requires the shipped scaler not to move; `:352-424` plants six real leaks — including an Ebola scaler fit on support+query and an acausal support cell — and requires every gate to fire. `models/windows.py:29-33` zero-left-pads short windows with the comment naming the exact bug avoided ("a negative start index would slice from the TAIL"), and `:36-40` decides an example's phase by the *target* column only. `bundles.py:228-243` proves LOCF is causal by perturbing a later observation and requiring earlier cells not to move.

**Every comparator scores bit-identical cells, verified from artifacts.** For both Ebola arms at all four horizons, the `h{h}__n` arrays from `encoder_ebola`, `encoder_ebola_zeroshot`, `naive__persistence` and `naive__support_mean` are all `np.array_equal` — 716/725/721/585 cells, same 18 origins. The L12-vs-L20 contrast is therefore a pure support-set contrast. The persistence floor was also stress-tested: it emits 0 at unobserved sources for 15.9–25.2% of scored pairs, and rebuilding it with an LOCF source moves country-macro RMSE 43.41→41.90 (h3), 50.68→51.54 (h15) — the h15 encoder win survives either floor.

**The metric primitives are adversarially self-checked.** `score.py:353-379` checks exactly the cases that would silently produce a plausible wrong table: WIS hand-computed to 0.28 and invariant to crossed input order; WIS == CRPS == MAE for a degenerate forecast (which is what lets a deterministic floor enter a WIS table); sMAPE 0/0 excluded rather than scored as perfect, with the discriminating case [0,0,5] vs [0,0,3] = 50 not 16.67; nRMSE NaN at zero level. The WIS formula matches Bracher et al. including the `1/(K+1/2)` normalisation and `w_k = α_k/2` (`score.py:159-180`), and CRPS is explicitly labelled a biased 5-quantile approximation (`score.py:183-189`). The bootstrap engine is verified against brute force (`analysis.py:271-304`) and the n=5 Wilcoxon is printed as "(n=5, supporting)" — correctly, since scipy's exact two-sided floor at n=5 is p=0.0625 and can never reach 0.05.

**The conformal variant choice was argued from this project's arithmetic, not from fashion.** Split conformal needs n≥9 for a finite 90% interval; the primary arm has 48/38/18/0 pairs, so h3 splits to ~16 and h15 has nothing to split (`Priority_Fix_Progress.md:173-180`). EnbPI is rejected because zero target-disease data enters training, so there are no out-of-bag residuals (`:182-185`). `conformal.py:42-49` states there is **no** finite-sample guarantee on Ebola and quotes ACI's worst case (α₁+γ)/(Tγ) = 0.167 at T=18 — the correct reading of Gibbs & Candès, said before a reviewer has to say it. The layer was registered as amendment A7/§5b *before* scoring, and the frozen config digest (2026-08-10 20:06) predates the first scored Ebola record (22:05).

**The one-way-door guards actually guard.** C8 asserts before data is loaded and is proved by a control that requires the AssertionError (`train/loop.py:506-516`); `results_paths.py:69-70` returns "misc" for any filename containing "smoke" *before* consulting the prefix table, so `--dry-run` structurally cannot write into the scored record path; `train/lodo.py:900-903` refuses a variant-patience run under the default prefix; `train/joint.py:299-325` asserts a one-dataset loss leaves every other adapter's grad exactly `None`, with a positive control. `conformal.py:449-453` even records that a fixture once "passed for the wrong reason" and was fixed.

**Seed discipline held under schedule pressure.** An earlier cut of HeatGNN to 3 seeds was reversed once cost was measured (`decisions.md:166-169`), and `Meta_Learning_Decision.md:188-190` rules "Cut arms, never seeds" with the t-critical argument (2.78 → 4.30). Every results family on disk holds 5 seeds.

## What went wrong

**No finding survived at CRITICAL.** No headline claim currently on disk is invalidated by a defect. That is partly because the two most exposed claims — negative cross-disease transfer, and "the pre-registered criterion was not met" — survive every test run against them. The failures below change numbers, block claims, or make the write-up unsafe.

### M1 — The Ebola headline and its confidence interval are different statistics

**What.** The reported number is a NODE-AVERAGED country-macro; the pre-registered CI is a CELL-POOLED country-macro. On Ebola RMSE they diverge 1.51×–2.02×.

**Evidence.** `score.py:255-278` `aggregate()` computes per-node metrics, then means within country, then over countries; `ebola_report.py:47` pins `FIELD="country_macro"`. `train/loop.py:378-388` stores node-POOLED sae/sse/n per (origin,country) and its own docstring warns "this is a CELL-POOLED country-macro… whereas the headline `aggregate()` is NODE-AVERAGED"; `analysis.py:71-90` rebuilds that quantity. Measured from `results/ebola/encoder_ebola__ebola_L12__seed42__{pernode,perorigin}.npz`: h3 RMSE 37.68 vs 74.33; h5 37.79 vs 75.08; h10 39.31 vs 72.57; h15 28.97 vs 44.01. Four of 64 skill cells flip sign, including one printed as `WIN` at `ebola_bootstrap.log:23` for a cell the headline table loses (41.19 against a 40.91 support_mean floor). `ebola_bootstrap.log:9` quotes the L12 few-shot h3 CI as [38.349, 104.537] while the headline point is 38.20 — outside its own interval.

**Consequence.** `Ebola_Prereg.md:196-198` defines the pass criterion as few-shot beating persistence at h3 or h5 "with the confidence interval clearing zero". The point half and the interval half evaluate different estimands. The correct current verdict is **not adjudicable on the reported metric**: on the node-averaged headline, L12 few-shot h3/h5 beat persistence by +12.0% and +14.3% and no interval exists on that quantity; on the cell-pooled metric the CI spans zero. The mismatch is disclosed generically (`decisions.md:82-83` D5, `analysis.py:13-17`, `LDO3_Results.md:274`) but every disclosure is scoped "coincide on dense influenza, diverge on dengue" — Ebola appears in no divergent-panel list despite diverging harder than either.

**Fix.** Bootstrap the per-node scores directly from `results/ebola/*__pernode.npz` (read-only; no rescore) so the CI is on the node-averaged estimand. Until then no CI may be printed beside a `country_macro` figure, and `ebola_bootstrap.log` verdicts must be relabelled cell-pooled. Add Ebola to the divergent list at `analysis.py:14-15` and `train/loop.py:385`.

### M2 — Two incompatible significance rules disagree on 27 of 64 cells

**What.** `ebola_report.py` and `analysis.py` produce contradictory verdicts on the same cells, and each prices a different single variance component.

**Evidence.** Rule A, `ebola_report.py:91-99`: `within_noise = (min(imp) <= 0 <= max(imp))` — a pure order-statistic sign check over 5 seeds, pricing initialisation variance only. Rule B, `analysis.py:93-135`: percentile CI over 18 resampled origins built from SEED-AVERAGED sufficient stats (`analysis.py:62`, `np.mean(saes,0)`), carrying zero seed dispersion. Cross-tabulated: 18 cells printed with a signed skill percentage are n.s. under the bootstrap (e.g. L12 few-shot h3 RMSE: +12.0% "WIN" vs [-42.26, +6.95]); 8 cells printed "within noise" are significant **losses**, all on the L20 few-shot arm whose seed sd is 15.24/13.25/10.70 on means of 47.03/51.58/40.85.

**Consequence.** Which cells are "results" depends on which tool the reader opens, and the more optimistic one is the reader whose output feeds the results section. Eight real losses on the secondary arm read as noise.

**Fix.** Retire the order-statistic rule, or bootstrap origins *within* each seed and pool the five seed-level distributions. At minimum print both with the disagreement count, and never print "within noise" for a cell whose origin CI excludes zero. The project already did this reconciliation in prose for LDO3 (`LDO3_Results.md:277`); Ebola needs the same.

### M3 — Half of pre-registered E6 was never run, and running it flips two reported WINs

**What.** E6 requires a bootstrap "over districts and time origins" (`Ebola_Prereg.md:188-189`). Only origins are resampled.

**Evidence.** `analysis.py:65-68` draws origin indices only; `train/loop.py:264-281` collapses districts into 3 countries before writing `__perorigin.npz` ([18,3] for Ebola). Every block in `ebola_bootstrap.log` (lines 8, 41, 74, 107) is labelled "paired bootstrap over origins". The material exists: `results/ebola/*__pernode.npz` carries per-district mae/rmse/n_cells/country for all 20 records, with matched `results/naive/naive__ebola_L12__{persistence,support_mean}__pernode.npz`, so a paired district bootstrap is ~20 lines and no re-run. Running it: encoder MAE at h3 has an origin CI of [18.63, 31.57] versus a district-marginal CI of [13.07, 37.47]; `ebola_bootstrap.log:57` (L12 zero-shot h10 MAE vs support_mean, WIN [-2.412, -1.113]) becomes [-4.081, +0.045], and `:65` (h15 MAE vs support_mean, WIN [-1.404, -0.344]) becomes [-3.290, +0.850]. Both span zero.

**Consequence.** Two reported wins against the tougher floor do not survive the axis E6 named first. The persistence-based headline wins (h10 MAE [-11.08, -1.47], h15 MAE [-24.77, -4.00]) do survive. The gap is disclosed only in a progress summary (`Priority_Fix_Progress.md:766-770`), not in `decisions.md` or the prereg.

**Fix.** Add the district bootstrap off `__pernode.npz`, report it beside the origin CI, and downgrade the two support_mean cells. This is the same code change that fixes M1.

### M4 — The artifact that records the pre-registered verdict cannot be regenerated and is untracked

**What.** No committed code can produce `results/reports/ebola_bootstrap.log`, and the criterion logic inside it is unversioned — and demonstrably wrong.

**Evidence.** `analysis.py:38` hardcodes `NAIVES = ("persistence","seasonal","train_mean")` and `:105-106` loads all three with no `exists()` guard (unlike `gate_reads` at `:152-153`), while `results/naive/` correctly holds only persistence and support_mean for Ebola per A5 (`train/ebola.py:18-19`). `bootstrap_ci` (`:93-94`) has no `naives` parameter; `:314` restricts `--dataset` to `choices=DEV`, which excludes both arms. The log's rows match `analysis.py:117-118`/`:132-133` byte-for-byte and add two sections `analysis.py` never emits (`:136`, `:147`). A repo-wide grep for "ebola_bootstrap" returns nothing; `git ls-files results/reports/` is empty (`.gitignore:20` is `/results/`). The criterion logic is already wrong: `ebola_bootstrap.log:147` claims to list "EVERY CELL WHERE THE CI CLEARS ZERO AGAINST PERSISTENCE" but omits `ebola_L20` few-shot h3 MAE, CI [1.454, 11.386] (`:80`), which clears zero in the losing direction. It is a wins-only list under a clears-zero label.

**Consequence.** The numbers are re-derivable with a one-line override, so this is not a lost result — but which arm is primary, which horizons count and which cells get listed exist nowhere in reviewable code, and the one instance we can inspect is biased.

**Fix.** Parameterise `NAIVES` on `bootstrap_ci`, lift `choices=DEV`, commit the criterion driver, re-run, and diff against the archived log before quoting either. Git-track the decision-bearing logs or copy them into `progress/outcomes/`.

### M5 — Every calibrated Ebola coverage figure consumes future truth

**What.** The online ACI loop applies the h-step outcome of origin *t* to the alpha used at origin *t+1*, with no lag.

**Evidence.** `conformal.py:347-355` keys each stream entry for origin t to truth at `raw[m, t+h]`; `conformal.py:160-171` updates `a_t` after every entry. Ebola origins are 18 consecutive weeks `t ∈ [19,36]` (`Ebola_Prereg.md:86-92`), so at h=3/5/10/15 the alpha used at t+1 depends on an observation made 2/4/9/14 weeks after that forecast would have issued. This is the documented delayed-feedback problem in multi-step online conformal prediction. Undisclosed: `Ebola_Prereg.md:246-248` constraint 4 requires such truth consumption to be entered in the amendment log; the log ends at A7 with no entry, and `Priority_Fix_Progress.md:461` asserts compliance.

**Consequence.** Concentrated rather than universal — the `+λ` static column is unaffected (frozen cross-disease λ_h, no Ebola truth), and in most cells `+ACI` is within thousandths of it. But L12 few-shot h15 lifts from static 0.464–0.782 to 0.794–0.829, and several h5 cells move 4–11 points. Under an honest lag-h stream, h15 has 3 of 18 origins with feedback available, so 15/18 run at α₁ and L12 few-shot h15 lands near 0.69–0.71, ~0.20 off nominal. Three reported items change: the `+ACI` column at `Priority_Fix_Progress.md:696-712`, the "mean absolute deviation 0.039 / 0.045" summary, and the claim at `:721` that no deviation breached the T=18 bound of 0.167.

**Fix.** Re-run `--apply` with an h-lagged ACI (update at t using outcomes from origins ≤ t−h) and report both columns; at h15 that leaves ~3 usable feedback steps, which is itself the honest finding. Failing a re-run, relabel the column "retrospective / oracle-feedback" everywhere.

### M6 — The pre-registered Ebola UQ block is not delivered

**What.** WIS, CRPS and PIT are implemented and hand-checked but never computed on any Ebola archive.

**Evidence.** `Ebola_Prereg.md:192-194` (E6) requires them for both arms from archived quantiles; `Review Doc.md:77` demands them by name. `score.py:167-230` implements all of them with self-checks at `:361-379`. Their only consumer is `diagnostics/ldo3_report.py:380-405`, driven over the LDO3 dev folds. For Ebola the sole quantile consumer is `conformal.py:333-376`, which reads `q[m,k,0]` and `q[m,k,-1]` and emits 90% coverage/width only — confirmed at `results/reports/conformal_apply.log:6`. The 50% interval is absent too, though `score.py` computes it free from the same 5-level grid. All 20 quantile archives exist.

**Consequence.** G4 is REQUIRED (`Final Internal Project Brief.md:32`) and the required metric set is complete only on development folds, not on the headline case study. A hub-literate reviewer asks for WIS first. Disclosed at `Priority_Fix_Progress.md:771-773` and `PROJECT.md:38`.

**Fix.** Add an Ebola branch to the `ldo3_report` UQ path over `results/ebola/*__quantiles.npz` for both arms × both regimes × 50% and 90%. This is a read over frozen files and is explicitly protocol-legal — A7 (`Ebola_Prereg.md:225`) was registered so these stay computable post hoc.

### M7 — The Ebola cumulative envelope discards cells on a premise that is false for 69% of them

**What.** `to_schema.py:228-231` states that where a cumulative report falls it is "a single-week data-entry dropout, not a downward revision." Measured on the pinned xlsx (`build_datasets.py:44`, sha256 `2d679a31…`): of 368 downward steps — exactly the 368 cells `data_audit.md:1143` reports removing — only 115 (31%) return to the prior high-water mark within three weeks.

**Evidence.** `sierra leone|western area rural` reports 2,257 on 2014-12-27 then an internally consistent 1,299 → 1,545 across thirteen weeks, all masked; the file's own `New cases` column records nonzero incidence in 6 of those 13 weeks. `liberia|lofa` reports 822 then a consistent 623 → 679 branch across seventeen trailing weeks. Thirteen districts terminate in ≥4 consecutive masked weeks. In the scored target window (cols 22–51 from `bundles.origins()`), `liberia|lofa` retains 7 of 30 weeks and `guinea|gueckedou` 17 of 30 — two of the three endpoints `loaders/ebola_load.py:27-29,:86-89` asserts as the outbreak's critical transmission pathway. The mass gate (`to_schema.py:256`, `tests/test_leakage.py:246`) bounds released mass only from above (`got > want + 0.5`), so suppression is invisible by construction.

**Consequence.** No released number is arithmetically wrong and the aggregate loss is disclosed. What is undisclosed is that the stated justification holds for under a third of the discarded cells, the per-district distribution of the loss (which `Review Doc.md:55` records a standing client request for), and that a re-basing treatment of level shifts — foreclosed by the false premise, never evaluated — would restore roughly 24 of Lofa's 30 target weeks. Corrections to note: the loss is *not* concentrated in the largest districts (top-10 hold 77 of 368 masked weeks) nor in the decline phase (21.8% early vs 21.1% late), and case *mass* is preserved.

**Fix.** Distinguish dropout from level shift before masking: a fall followed by a sustained internally monotone lower branch is a revision, and should be re-baselined. At minimum publish a per-district masked-week and trailing-run table in `data_audit.md` §3.5, and add a gate that fails on a long trailing masked run so the loss is loud.

Note in the Ebola methods that the trunk includes a panel that loses to a train-mean baseline by 61/131/115% at h5/h10/h15 (`LDO3_Results.md:217-218`, `Doubt.md:683-693`).

### M9 — Two of twelve country-macro slots are statistically empty

**What.** `score.py:274` averages per-country values with no minimum-node or minimum-cell floor, and `score.py:247` drops every node constant over its *scored* cells.

**Evidence.** Reproduced on `data/processed/dengue.npz` and `results/single/encoder__dengue__seed42__pernode.npz`: 559 excluded, 6,161 scored, 12 countries. Japan holds 1 of 47 nodes (`japan|tokyo`, 94 cells, mean 0.064, max 4); Argentina 20 of 275 (234 cells, median 11.5). Two of twelve equal slots rest on 328 of 403,074 scored cells (0.08%). Live in the headline: `encoder__dengue__seed42.json` records country_macro RMSE 49.977 vs node_mean 23.055.
